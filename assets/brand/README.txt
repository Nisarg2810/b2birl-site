B2B IRL logo kit
================
Colours: Ink #1E1E1C · Ivory #FCF7F1 · House yellow #F2BF1D (seal ring only)
Type: Cormorant Garamond Medium (wordmark), IBM Plex Mono (tagline). All text is outlined.

wordmark/  "B2B IRL" with or without IN REAL LIFE. Default logo for headers, documents, decks.
seal/      The circular seal. Use alone where the wordmark is too wide: stamps, stickers, invitations.
lockup/    Seal + wordmark. Horizontal for wide spaces, stacked for square ones.
icon/      App / browser icons and favicons (1024, 512, 192, 180 apple-touch, 32, 16).
social/    Profile pictures (safe inside a circle crop) and the 1200x630 link-share card.

-ink    = dark logo, transparent background (use on light backgrounds)
-ivory  = light logo, transparent background (use on dark backgrounds or photos)
-on-ivory / -on-ink = logo with its background included

Rules: leave clear space equal to the letter height on every side. Never stretch, outline,
shadow or recolour. Only the seal's dotted outer ring may be house yellow.
