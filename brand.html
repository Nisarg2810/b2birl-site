Brand guidelines

The house style.

How B2B IRL looks, sounds and moves. For our team, our hosts and our partners.

01 · Essence

B2B marketing, in real life.

A house for the people who do the work. Small rooms, honest conversations, people you will keep.

Quiet

We never shout. No exclamation marks, no countdown clocks, no "last chance". Confidence is calm.

Honest

Real numbers over highlight reels. We say what worked and what didn't, and so do our guests.

Selective

Chosen, not ticketed. Every seat is offered personally. Scarcity is earned, never faked.

Warm

Premium, never cold. A long table, a good chair, someone who remembers your name.

02 · Wordmark & seal

The wordmark is set, never drawn: Cormorant Garamond Medium, tracked wide, with IN REAL LIFE beneath in Plex Mono. The seal signs our invitations and nothing else.

Give it room

Clear space on every side equal to the height of the letters. Nothing touches the wordmark.

Never decorate

No outlines, shadows, gradients, rotation or colour other than ink and ivory. The seal's outer ring may take the house colour.

06 · Photography & motion

Real rooms, real light.

People, mid-conversation

Do

Tables, light, detail

Do

The city, quietly

Do

Stages, crowds, lanyards

Don't
Grade

Slightly warm, slightly desaturated. Never filters, never black and white for effect.

Motion

Slow, muted loops of 10 to 20 seconds. No text burned into video. Always a pause button, and stillness for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

People first

Our own guests, with their consent, will always beat stock. Replace stock as each room happens.

Never

Handshakes, headsets, laptops with logos, stage wides, swag, or anything that looks like a trade show.
