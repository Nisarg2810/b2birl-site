Privacy

How we handle your information.

Last updated 31 October 2026

What we collect

This website does not use accounts, analytics or advertising cookies. When you request an invitation, subscribe or write to us, our forms open your own email app with your details written out. We receive only what you choose to send to hello@b2birl.com.

How we use it

To consider your request, to reply to you, to run B2B IRL events, and to send the letter if you asked for it. That is all.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We never sell your information, and we never share guest lists or contact details with partners or hosts. If a partner would like to meet you, we ask you first.

Third parties that load on this site

Fonts load from Google Fonts, photographs from Unsplash and videos from Pexels. Like any website, those services receive the technical information your browser sends when it loads their files, such as your IP address.

At our events

A photographer works at our events. Tell us, or wear the lanyard that asks us not to, and we will not photograph you. Sessions are never recorded.

Your choices

Write to hello@b2birl.com and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it.
